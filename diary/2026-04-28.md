# 2026-04-28

Yesterday night temperatures reached 2 degrees. Stayed in a cottage with a stunning lake view of Pangong Lake (the famous 3 Idiots location). 

The hotel host was awesome; his effort to make us comfortable was really appreciative. The food was good and the tea was awesome—in fact, the tea throughout the entire trip has been consistently good. He even arranged a cake for a couple's first anniversary.

During the campfire, everyone shared their stories. I shared mine too, though I could have perhaps avoided talking about the fight, but that's fine.
